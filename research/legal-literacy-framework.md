# Legal Literacy Framework

PLKO evaluates public legal literacy across **10 measurable indicators**:
1. **Source Accessibility**: Availability of official primary texts without paywalls or broken links.
2. **Content Readability**: Sentence length, vocabulary familiarity, and absence of unexplained jargon.
3. **Legal Concept Comprehension**: Citizen understanding of core rights and obligations.
4. **Procedure Comprehension**: Ability to sequence required steps and documents for a legal service.
5. **Official Service Identification**: Ability to distinguish official channels (e.g., `ecourt.mahkamahagung.go.id`, `sidbankum.kemenkum.go.id`) from unofficial intermediaries.
6. **Information Reliability Discrimination**: Ability to spot unverified or outdated legal claims.
7. **Counsel Referral Recognition**: Knowing when a matter requires an advocate, OBH/LBH, or paralegal.
8. **User-Perceived Usefulness**: Anonymous usefulness and clarity ratings (`Dataset 10`).
9. **Content Freshness**: Time elapsed since last verification against `Dataset 11`.
10. **Primary Source Traceability**: Percentage of substantive claims linked to verified `source_id` and `provision_id`.
