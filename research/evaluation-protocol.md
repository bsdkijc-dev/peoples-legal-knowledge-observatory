# Evaluation Protocol

## 1. Non-Conflation Principle
Page views, downloads, and social media engagement **do not** measure legal literacy. Comprehension must be evaluated using validated assessment items (`Dataset 09`) and structured user feedback (`Dataset 10`).

## 2. Causal Claims Restriction
PLKO does not claim causal impact on legal outcomes or literacy gains without a pre-registered, ethically approved evaluation design (e.g., pre/post comprehension testing with informed consent).

## 3. Ethical Safeguards
- No survey or comprehension instrument may be deployed without explicit human approval (`P4`, `P5`).
- All participant feedback is anonymized (`anonymous_user_category`) with explicit `consent_status`.
