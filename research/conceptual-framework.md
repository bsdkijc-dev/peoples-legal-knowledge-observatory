# Conceptual Framework

PLKO models public legal empowerment as a seven-stage chain connecting institutional law to citizen capability:

1. **Official Legal Sources** — Authoritative primary texts (`peraturan.bpk.go.id`, `jdihn.go.id`, `mahkamahagung.go.id`, `mkri.id`, `bphn.go.id`).
2. **Source Verification** — Confirming authenticity, promulgation, effective date, amendments, repeals, and judicial review status.
3. **Legal Knowledge Extraction** — Decomposing statutes into atomic provisions (`Dataset 02`) with explicit scope and temporal bounds.
4. **Plain-Language Transformation** — Translating provisions into clear Bahasa Indonesia (`Dataset 03`–`08`) tailored to differentiated literacy and connectivity contexts.
5. **Human Legal Review** — Independent substantive legal and accessibility validation (`review-protocol.md`).
6. **Public Legal Knowledge** — Multi-format distribution (web, low-bandwidth HTML, printable PDFs, audio scripts, screen-reader text).
7. **Legal Literacy Evaluation** — Measuring comprehension, service-identification capability, and feedback (`Dataset 09`–`10`) without conflating page views with comprehension.
