# Research Questions — PLKO

## Primary Research Question
> **Can every Indonesian citizen access, understand, and use reliable legal information to protect their rights?**

## Sub-Questions
1. **Source Accessibility**: To what extent are primary Indonesian legal sources (statutes, implementing regulations, Supreme Court rules, Constitutional Court rulings) publicly accessible, machine-readable, and up-to-date on official portals?
2. **Comprehensibility**: How readable and understandable are plain-language legal transformations compared to original statutory text across Indonesia's 10 target population groups?
3. **Traceability & Accuracy**: Can an AI-assisted pipeline maintain 100% deterministic traceability from every plain-language claim back to verified statutory provisions while preventing legal hallucinations?
4. **Literacy & Actionability**: Do structured procedure guides, FAQs, and offline/audio materials measurably improve citizens' ability to identify official services, distinguish reliable from unreliable legal information, and recognize when professional legal assistance is needed?
