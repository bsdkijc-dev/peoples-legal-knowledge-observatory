# Changelog

All notable changes to the People's Legal Knowledge Observatory (PLKO) will be documented in this file.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-23
### Added
- Initial SAFE MODE repository structure (`peoples-legal-knowledge-observatory/`).
- Governance suite (`governance/`): research charter, legal accuracy, source, privacy, publication, AI governance, and human review protocols.
- Research framework (`research/`): research questions, conceptual framework, methodology, legal literacy framework, and evaluation protocol.
- 12 JSON Schemas (`schemas/`) covering Datasets 01–12.
- Authoritative source register (`sources/source-register.csv`) and verification assessment (`sources/source-verification.md`).
- Synthetic sample datasets (`data/synthetic/`) for end-to-end pipeline testing.
- Source traceability validator (`src/validation/traceability_validator.py`) and automated test suite (`tests/`).
- Phase 2 initial 10 legal knowledge topics and sample plain-language drafts flagged for human legal review.
