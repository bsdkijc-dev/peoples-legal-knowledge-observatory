# Publication Policy

## 1. Publication Classifications
- `INTERNAL_DRAFT`: Unreviewed AI-assisted or researcher drafts.
- `SYNTHETIC_TEST`: Synthetic records used solely for schema and pipeline validation (never cited as real legal evidence).
- `APPROVED_PUBLIC`: Content that has passed Source Verification, Legal Review, Accessibility Review, and Human Publication Approval.

## 2. Mandatory Human Approval Gates
Human approval is strictly required before:
1. Publishing legal explanations or procedural guidance.
2. Making claims about current legal status.
3. Deploying surveys, interviews, or collecting personal data.
4. Contacting external institutions or individuals.
5. Uploading datasets to GitHub or Kaggle.
6. Replacing previously published legal information.

## 3. Licensing & Redistribution
Before publishing any full-text legal document on GitHub or Kaggle, verify copyright, redistribution terms, and source conditions. By default, PLKO publishes **structured metadata, provenance registers, synthetic examples, and original plain-language summaries** with links to official portals rather than bulk-republishing third-party PDFs.
