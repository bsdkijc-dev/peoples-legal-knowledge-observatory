# Legal Accuracy Policy

## 1. Core Accuracy Rules
1. Every substantive legal statement must be grounded in a verified primary legal source (`source_id` + specific `provision_id` or article/paragraph reference).
2. Clearly distinguish **binding legal rules** (statutes, government regulations, Supreme Court regulations) from **explanatory commentary** and **hypothetical examples**.
3. Distinguish **legislation** (UU, PP, Perpres, Perma) from **court decisions** (Putusan MA / Putusan MK); never treat a single lower-court judgment as a universally binding rule.
4. Always state the **effective date** and **amendment/judicial review status** of the cited legal rule.
5. Explicitly explain legal uncertainty, transitional provisions, and conflicting authorities.
6. Never invent procedural deadlines, court fees, or administrative requirements.
7. Never promise legal outcomes or generate individualized legal advice.
8. Never expose personal information (PII) from judicial documents.

## 2. Handling Unverified or Conflicting Sources
- **Unverified Sources**: Must be marked `UNVERIFIED` and blocked from public release.
- **Conflicting Sources**: Must be flagged `CONFLICT_FLAGGED` and escalated for human legal review (`review-protocol.md`).
