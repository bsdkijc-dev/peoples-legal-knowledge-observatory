# Privacy & Data Protection Policy

PLKO aligns its data handling with **UU No. 27 Tahun 2022 tentang Pelindungan Data Pribadi (UU PDP)** and strict research ethics:

1. **Judicial Document Anonymization**: Never expose names, addresses, national IDs (NIK), phone numbers, or sensitive personal data of litigants, witnesses, victims, or court personnel in public datasets or plain-language examples.
2. **Feedback & Evaluation Privacy**: Dataset 10 (`Legal Knowledge Feedback`) stores only `anonymous_user_category` and `consent_status`. No IP addresses, names, or contact details may be collected without explicit, documented human-approved consent protocols.
3. **Hypothetical Examples Only**: All practical illustrations in plain-language guides must use clearly labeled hypothetical personas (e.g., *Contoh Hipotetis: Ibu Sari*).
4. **No Unauthorized Scraping**: Do not scrape restricted portals or bypass authentication/rate limits.
