# Research Charter — People's Legal Knowledge Observatory (PLKO)

**Project:** People's Legal Knowledge Observatory (PLKO)  
**Tagline:** Legal Knowledge for Everyone. Justice Without Barriers.  
**Parent Initiative:** MA Open Justice & Public Prosperity Initiative 2027–2030  
**Status:** Independent research and public-interest knowledge project.  
**Effective Date:** 2026-11-23

## 1. Independence & Non-Endorsement Statement
PLKO is an independent, public-interest research initiative. PLKO does **not** represent, speak for, or claim official endorsement or authorization from Mahkamah Agung RI, Mahkamah Konstitusi RI, Badan Pembinaan Hukum Nasional (BPHN), Badan Pemeriksa Keuangan (BPK), or any other government institution.

## 2. Core Research Question
> *Can every Indonesian citizen access, understand, and use reliable legal information to protect their rights?*

## 3. Scope & Target Audiences
PLKO serves 10 population groups with differentiated literacy, connectivity, and accessibility needs:
1. General public
2. Rural communities
3. Micro and small enterprises (UMKM)
4. Workers and informal workers
5. Women and families
6. Older persons
7. Persons with disabilities
8. Students and educators
9. Community legal assistance organizations (OBH/LBH/paralegals)
10. Village-level public service providers

## 4. Non-Negotiable Safeguards
- **SAFE MODE Operation**: No public release of legal explanations, procedural guides, or datasets without explicit human legal review and approval.
- **No Legal Advice**: PLKO provides general public legal literacy and procedural orientation, never individualized legal advice or outcome guarantees.
- **Full Traceability**: Every legal proposition must link to an authoritative, verified primary legal source.
